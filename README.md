# Shadow Nights

## About the Game

**Shadow Nights** is an action bullet-hell platformer that combines challenging platforming with fast-paced combat and time manipulation abilities.

Players must navigate dangerous environments, avoid enemy attacks, and use different time-based abilities to gain an advantage in combat. These abilities allow players to control the flow of battle, creating opportunities to dodge attacks and overcome challenging encounters.

## Gameplay Overview

Shadow Nights combines two main gameplay elements:

- **Platforming:** Navigate through environments while overcoming obstacles and avoiding hazards.
- **Bullet-Hell Combat:** Dodge incoming projectiles and enemy attacks while finding opportunities to strike back.

Players are encouraged to make use of their abilities strategically, especially when facing multiple enemies or large amounts of incoming projectiles.

## Time Manipulation Abilities

The player has access to several abilities that manipulate time:

| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| Screen Time Slow | Slows down time across the entire screen, giving the player more time to react to enemies and projectiles. |
| Localized Time Stop | Creates a circular area that slows down enemies and projectiles within its range. |
| Self Rewind | Rewinds the player's own actions, allowing them to reverse their movement through time. |

These abilities provide different ways to approach combat and platforming challenges.

## Target Audience

Shadow Nights is aimed at players who enjoy action platformers, challenging combat, and exploration.

The game is designed with experienced and patient players in mind, particularly those who enjoy:

- Challenging enemy encounters and combat.
- Dodging complex projectile patterns.
- Exploring platforming environments.
- Learning and mastering different abilities.
- Overcoming difficult gameplay challenges.

## Inspirations

Shadow Nights takes inspiration from popular platformers and Metroidvania games that combine challenging gameplay, exploration, and unique mechanics.

### Touhou Luna Nights
- Released in 2019.
- A Japanese Metroidvania game known for its time manipulation mechanics and action-based gameplay.

### Celeste
- Released in 2018.
- A challenging platformer focused on precise movement and skill-based gameplay.

### Hollow Knight
- Released in 2017.
- A Metroidvania game featuring exploration, challenging enemies, and interconnected environments.

### Hollow Knight: Silksong
- Released in 2025.
- A follow-up to Hollow Knight, continuing its focus on exploration and challenging action-platforming gameplay.
